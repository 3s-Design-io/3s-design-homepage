What

When

2023

Why

Innoneer ?

This is the new Branding design for Innoneer, a new Vtuber group. The main concept is with their name, "In-and-on-air". Innoneer is a new Vtuber group based in Hong Kong, their branding concept is to create a platform to let everyone come in and go on stream.

Graphics style and Color tone.

To extend the concept of "platform", we found we could base on the brand name "Innoneer" to become "In n(and) on eer(Air)". So that the Visual identity we've designed a symbol system of a small dot going everywhere he/she wants, representing every Vtuber can do whatever he/she wants freedomly on this platform. And for the color tone, since it is a young and smooth new brand aiming at young teenagers and Innoneer's Vtuber is very cute and lovely, pastel pink is a good solution.
