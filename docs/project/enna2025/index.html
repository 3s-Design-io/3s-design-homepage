Selected project

Enna Alouette Travel in Hong Kong

Back to Index ↗

Information

What

When

2025

Why

This travel-themed design for Enna Alouette’s Hong Kong event features a soft, "Kawaii" scrapbook aesthetic that feels both nostalgic and modern. The color palette is dominated by pastel rainbows and gradients, utilizing a distinctive mosaic-tile background that subtly references Hong Kong’s iconic MTR station walls.

Graphics style and Color tone.

The composition is densely packed with playful sticker-style icons, including dim sum, airplanes, and local landmarks, all featuring thick white offsets to create a physical "pop-up" effect. The typography is whimsical and bubbly, with layered outlines in pink, blue, and white that ensure readability against the busy background. This "memory book" style is further enhanced by the inclusion of Polaroid-style photo frames and travel ephemera like tickets and stamps. Overall, the design successfully blends Enna’s elegant character art with a lighthearted, souvenir-inspired layout that captures the joy of exploration.
