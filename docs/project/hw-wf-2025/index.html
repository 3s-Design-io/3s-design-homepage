High West - Whisky Festival 2025 ?

High West Distillery took center stage at the Whisky Festival 2025 Hong Kong, showcasing their award-winning craft spirits. Known for their unique blending and high-elevation aging in Park City, Utah, High West brought the spirit of the American West to the city, offering enthusiasts a deep dive into the complexity of barrel-aged rye and bourbon.

Graphics style and Color tone.

The booth was designed to transport guests directly to the distillery’s barrel house. We used a high-resolution photographic backdrop of authentic High West whisky barrels to create an immersive, atmospheric tasting environment. To ensure high visibility across the festival hall, a custom-designed neon sign served as a modern, vibrant focal point, blending the brand’s rustic heritage with a sharp, contemporary edge.
