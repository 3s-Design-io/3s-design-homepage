Four Pillars - Wine & Dine 2025 ?

The Hong Kong Wine & Dine Festival 2025 is the city's flagship culinary event, drawing hundreds of thousands of visitors to the Central Harbourfront. Representing Four Pillars Gin, our goal was to translate the brand’s award-winning Australian spirit into a high-energy, festival-scale experience. Amidst a sea of exhibitors, we focused on creating a destination that felt less like a booth and more like a vibrant late-night bar.

Graphics style and Color tone.

The design strategy was built around Maximum Luminosity and Interactive Engagement. Knowing the festival peaks after sunset, we prioritized light-based design elements to ensure the booth remained a beacon on the event floor. Key design features included: Kinetic Interaction (The Lucky Wheel): We integrated a fully functional, branded lucky wheel to drive foot traffic and create a "theatrical" moment at the counter. This gamified the tasting experience, encouraging crowd participation and boosting direct sales. Integrated LED & Neon Signage: To dominate the nighttime visual field, we utilized custom neon brand marks and extensive LED lighting. This not only highlighted the signature purple and orange palette but also ensured the bottle displays were high-contrast and "Instagrammable." High-Impact Visual Saturation: Every square inch of the booth was utilized to reinforce brand identity, from the glowing overhead headers to the backlit cocktail menus, creating a cohesive, immersive environment that drove record-breaking engagement.
