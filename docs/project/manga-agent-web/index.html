When

Why

Manga Agent Website Design ?

Recently, I worked on a major project, collaborating with Manga Agent to redesign their official merchandise website. The site underwent a complete transformation from their old template to a more colorful, rounded, modern, and streamlined design, incorporating the unique brand colors of Manga Agent. At the same time, we integrated some features from the official websites of their main products. This makes it easier for users to switch between the official site and this one, ensuring a seamless experience.

Graphics style and Color tone.

The website thoughtfully categorizes different main official brands and further divides them into artist profiles. Additionally, it now features a section where users can view all official and fan-made merchandise of a specific artist at once. This was a key update to help users quickly find all the products related to their favorite artists and easily spend their money. Another significant update is the introduction of a tagging system. One of the issues with the old website was that users couldn't quickly identify if a product was official or fan-made. With the new tagging system, products are clearly labeled as official or fan-made, along with additional tags like creator group, character name, re-release, autograph, and more. Clicking on these tags allows users to quickly jump to different product searches, making it more convenient to find what they're looking for and add items to their cart.
