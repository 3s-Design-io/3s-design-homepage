Why

Bay 247?

Bay 247 is a new concept of an indoor golf simulator sports room. The aim is to be friendly, and clean, but with more environmental decor related to the golf-related symbols.

Graphics style and Color tone.

The main graphics element and graphics are golf and flat style, the aim of each graphic is means to be reusable on various platforms such as posters, DM, wall deco, and everywhere. The main color tones are green and orange as the highlight color. The green means the mountain and the orange dot mean the sun. Simply create a feeling of "we golf anywhere, anytime."
