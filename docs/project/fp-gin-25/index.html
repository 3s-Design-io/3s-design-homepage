Four Pillars - Ginsanity 2025 ?

We took the Four Pillars Gin experience to the center stage of Ginsanity HK 2025, Hong Kong's ultimate gin festival. Celebrating the best of artisanal Australian distilling, Four Pillars brought their multi-award-winning range to thousands of local enthusiasts. The mission was to create an immersive, graphic-heavy environment that reflected the brand’s modern, high-energy personality within a bustling festival atmosphere.

Graphics style and Color tone.

The booth design maximized the small, 3x3 festival tent footprint by leaning heavily on Bold Chromaticism. We deployed the brand’s signature clash of vibrant orange and electric purple across every surface—from the custom backdrop to the table coverings—ensuring maximum visibility across the festival floor. Key design features included: Theatrical Graphics: The backdrop utilized a chaotic, pop-art style collage of oranges, sun rays, and floating cocktails. This provided a dynamic, photogenic backdrop for festival-goers. The "Bloody" Neon Anchor: To highlight the star product (Bloody Shiraz Gin), we created a custom, two-color neon sign as the booth's centerpiece. This modern, glowing artifact became the primary visual anchor and a "must-snap" moment for social media. Functional Articulation: Every element, including the POS menus, wooden display crates, and branded "Next Morning Bar" caps, was styled to fit this high-octane, graphic aesthetic, ensuring the brand experience was seamless and unforgettable.
