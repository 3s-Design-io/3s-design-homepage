Tokoyami Towa - 1st EP "Scream" Wallpaper

This is a set(6) of fan-made artwork for the Virtual YouTuber Tokoyami Towa, to congrats her birthday. Some of them I have put some crazy ideas in it, although it might seem crazy but it is fun. The results are pretty good and I learn a few more Design technic and tricks from it. It is a fun journey.

What

When

2022

Why

Tokoyami Towa - 1st EP "Scream" Wallpaper?

Graphic styles and Color tone.

To respect the original Art Direction and to understand each song to create a Key visual of each song and make it as wallpaper. The main color tone is purple from the original design, the purple are representing the character and means to bring the feeling of mystery and charm.
