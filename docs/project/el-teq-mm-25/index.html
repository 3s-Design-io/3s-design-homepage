El Tequileno - Margarita Mile 2025 ?

Margarita Mile 2025 was a high-profile city-wide activation in Hong Kong, celebrating the craftsmanship of El Tequileño Tequila. The event challenged cocktail enthusiasts to explore the city’s top bars, collecting stamps at each stop for a chance to win exclusive prizes. I was responsible for the full design suite, creating a cohesive visual ecosystem that guided participants from their first drink to the final lucky draw.

Graphics style and Color tone.

The visual strategy for Margarita Mile was focused on Gamified Exploration. We needed the materials to be durable enough for a night out, yet stylish enough to be kept as souvenirs. Key design features included: The "Margarita Map" & Passport: I designed a comprehensive, easy-to-navigate map and stamp-collection passport. The layout balanced technical clarity with the brand's sun-drenched, agave-inspired aesthetic, making the journey through the city feel like an adventure. Custom Venue Menus: Each participating bar (such as Green Door and Courtroom) received customized menus. I maintained a consistent brand header while allowing the specific cocktail photography and venue details to shine, ensuring a seamless experience across different environments. Integrated Social & Print Assets: From promotional postcards to SNS-optimized graphics, the design utilized bold typography and high-contrast imagery to drive digital engagement and physical foot traffic simultaneously. Vibrant Visual Identity: We leaned into the signature "Sun Goddess" colors—burnt oranges, deep teals, and earthy ochres—to create a warm, inviting, and premium atmosphere across all physical and digital collateral.
