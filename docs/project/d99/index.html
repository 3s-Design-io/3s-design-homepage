Drinks99 - Visual Identity & Trade Assets

Back to Index ↗

What

When

2023-2026

Why

Drinks99 ?

Drinks99 is a premier luxury spirits distributor in Asia, supplying world-class hotels, Michelin-starred restaurants, and the "World’s 50 Best Bars" across Hong Kong, China, and Singapore. I was tasked with developing a cohesive Visual Identity that reflects their position as an industry leader. This comprehensive project included the design of the core brand deck, corporate stationery, and trade price lists, ensuring a consistent and high-impact brand presence across all B2B touchpoints.

Graphics style and Color tone.

The visual identity is built on a foundation of Sophisticated Contrast. We established a signature palette of Deep Black and Bold Red to immediately communicate luxury and authority. Key design features include: Purposeful Highlights: To contrast the clean, structured layouts, I introduced hand-drawn arrows and organic underlines in vibrant red. This adds a human, artisanal touch to the brand, much like a bartender’s personal recommendation. Modern Typography: The use of bold, condensed sans-serif typefaces ensures that key information—like brand names and pricing—is highly legible and commands attention. Imagery & Hierarchy: I utilized large, high-contrast photography of premium cocktails to evoke the sensory experience of the products, while maintaining a minimalist grid for technical data.
