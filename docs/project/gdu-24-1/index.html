Aimer Banner Design ?

A while ago, during Aimer's live concert in Hong Kong, I collaborated with the Aimer Hong Kong Fans Page to design a hand banner. I've designed several hand banners before, but this one is my favorite. I was thrilled to see that so many Aimer fans loved the design! On the day of the concert, it was a huge surprise to see everyone holding the banner, and I heard they ran out early! Some fans even thought it was an official product, haha. After the concert, I saw fans carrying the banner while getting takeout, which was really funny. I’m so glad everyone liked it. In the design, I incorporated elements of the sun, moon, and stars, with a subtle gradient in the background and used a special shimmery matte paper to give it a mysterious beauty!
