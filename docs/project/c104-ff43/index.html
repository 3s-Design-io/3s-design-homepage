C104 & FF43 - Dōjinshi book Designs

Back to Index ↗

Information

What

C103 & FF42 - Dōjinshi book Designs

When

2024

Why

C103 & FF42 carries forward the legacy of Japan & Taiwan’s premier Anime, Comics, and Games Event, with a special emphasis on the fan-made market. This iteration, I’ve had the pleasure of collaborating with 6 extraordinary artists to create a series of unique book covers, each a window into the artist’s soul. The 6 artists, with their distinct styles and artistic visions, have contributed to a diverse collection. The right art style for each book cover was paramount, ensuring that the spirit of their artistry is encapsulated and highlighted at C103 & FF42.

Graphic Style and Color Tone

The artistry involved in this project is as diverse as it is profound. Each artist brings to the table a distinct style, a unique palette of colors, and a personal touch that transforms each book cover and inner page into a visual symphony. As we gear up for C103 & FF42, we’re thrilled to present these eclectic designs, each telling its own story, each inviting the audience into a world crafted by the hands of a visionary. It’s more than just design.
