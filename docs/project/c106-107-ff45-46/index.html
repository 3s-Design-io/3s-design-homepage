Why

Comiket and Fancy Frontier is a vibrant Anime, Comics, and Games Event in Japan and Taiwan, where I’m immersed in the bustling fan-made market. This year, I’ve had the privilege of collaborating with different incredibly talented artists to craft unique book covers and inner page designs. The six artists, with their distinct styles and artistic visions, have contributed to a diverse collection. The right art style for each book cover was paramount, ensuring that the spirit of their artistry is encapsulated and highlighted at Comiket and Fancy Frontier.

Graphics style and Color tone.

The artistry involved in this project is as diverse as it is profound. Each artist brings to the table a distinct style, a unique palette of colors, and a personal touch that transforms each book cover and inner page into a visual symphony. As we gear up for Comiket and Fancy Frontier, we’re thrilled to present these eclectic designs, each telling its own story, each inviting the audience into a world crafted by the hands of a visionary. It’s more than just design.
