Why

Nakiri Ayame - 5th Birthday Project ?

This is a fan-made project for Nakiri Ayame, to congrats her 5th Birthday. In this project, we have a set of different events and different interactions. First, we have designed an MTR billboard to congrats her 5th Birthday, second, we have created an entire website to let the fans share congrats comments and photos, and lastly a fully painted bus. The billboard is located at HK Yau Ma Tei MTR.

Graphics style and Color tone.

In this project, I have taken the part in designing the event logo and the MTR billboard. In the design direction, it is important to respect the direction of the original character design which is traditional Japanese style. In the Event logo, I have picked a folding fan as a background and added a bunch of other elements that are related to making the composition feel filled and richly layered. In the MTR billboard, I used the same direction as the event logo and extended the graphic elements. Both logo and the MTR billboard's main color theme are gold and red, which will give a deep traditional Japanese style mood tone and contrast.
