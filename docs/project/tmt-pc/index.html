Why

Tokoyami Towa - TMT PC?

TMT = Towa Maji Tensh. This is a famous joke(or neta) in the fan communities, which means Towa's character is a Devil but always does something like an angel does. This is a fan-made project for myself, to congrats Virtual YouTuber Tokoyami Towa’s 2nd-anniversary debut event. This is my first time trying to put some glass stickers big scale like this(47x48cm). And the note to me is to do laser printing directly to the glass next time.

Graphics style and Color tone.

Towa’s Art Direction is a bit of Acid Graphics and Cyberpunk topic. It is interesting to try to mix these two strong, bold, cold graphics styles to contrast with her cuteness. The color tones I have designed to mainly use purple-pink, green, and black-gray only. The purple-pink and green are from the original character design, and the black-gray is the secondary color for contrast.
