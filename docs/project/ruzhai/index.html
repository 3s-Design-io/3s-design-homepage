Why

Ruzhai Branding Design ?

I recently developed a new brand identity for Mr. Ruzhai (@Ruzhai0609), using a natural, calm, and mature shade of dark green as the main color.

Graphics style and Color tone.

The logo retains Mr. Ruzhai's original deer head but has been simplified and modernized to convey a clean, organized, and refined feel. I also designed the business cards, combining gold foil with dark green to add a touch of maturity within a natural aesthetic.
