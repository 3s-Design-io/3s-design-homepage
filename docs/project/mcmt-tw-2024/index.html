miComet in TW - Birthday Project 2024

In honor of the 6th Anniversary and Birthday of beloved vtubers Sakura Miko and Hoshimachi Suisei, we present the miComet design collection. This captivating ensemble features key visuals meticulously crafted for promotion across Twitter, Facebook, and other social media platforms.

What

When

2024

Why

In honor of the 6th Anniversary and Birthday of beloved vtubers Sakura Miko and Hoshimachi Suisei, we present the miComet design collection. This captivating ensemble features key visuals meticulously crafted for promotion across Twitter, Facebook, and other social media platforms. But that’s not all! Our design magic extends beyond the digital realm. Imagine a colossal 26-ton LED screen truck adorned with the very same visuals. This mobile masterpiece will roam the bustling streets of Taipei, playing mesmerizing music videos of our cherished vtubers. For an entire month, from dawn till dusk, this luminous behemoth will weave through the cityscape, leaving a trail of awe and wonder.

Graphics style and Color tone.

To ensure consistency across various artworks—be it SNS ads, event announcements, or heartfelt acknowledgments—we’ve meticulously curated an artstyle that resonates. Picture a fusion of Neon-like Blue and Pink, a high-contrast palette that demands attention. These electrifying hues will leap off screens, captivating hearts and minds alike.
