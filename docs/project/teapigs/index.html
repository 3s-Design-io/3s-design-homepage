When

Why

teapigs ?

This is a comprehensive suite of digital and print assets for teapigs Hong Kong, a premium tea brand known for its "no-nonsense" quality and sustainable "tea temples." My role was to translate their vibrant UK-born identity into localized marketing materials, including the Trade Price List, promotional website banners, and seasonal sale campaign assets. By balancing clear information hierarchy with playful visuals, I ensured that both B2B clients and retail customers experienced a consistent and engaging brand journey.

Graphics style and Color tone.

The design follows a Playful Minimalist aesthetic that mirrors the brand's sustainable roots. Utilizing a craft-paper texture as the primary background, the style emphasizes an organic, "plastic-free" feel. Vibrant Color Coding: I implemented a bold, multi-color system to differentiate tea ranges (Core vs. Special), using the brand's signature hand-drawn illustrations to maintain a friendly and accessible tone. Dynamic Layouts: For the web banners, I used sunburst patterns and comic-style "pop" outlines to create energy and movement, making the promotions stand out against the neutral cardboard textures. Clear Typography: Using a chunky, sans-serif typeface with drop shadows, the information remains highly legible and professional while retaining a sense of fun and modernity.
