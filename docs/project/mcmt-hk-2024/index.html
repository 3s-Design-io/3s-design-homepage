miComet in HK Birthday Project 2024.

█ Project previewing -

This collection features a trio (or quartet) of lightbox designs celebrating the 6th Anniversary and Birthday of VTubers Sakura Miko and Hoshimachi Suisei. These lightboxes were showcased in March 2024, in collaboration with HK Virtual Idol Fanatics.

What

miComet in HK : Birthday Project 2024

When

2024

Why

Sakura Miko Lightbox Design

The centerpiece visual for this project was crafted by the talented しゃふぁさん, a fellow 35P member. It encapsulates the theme of ‘sakura in resplendent bloom.’ The design portrays the sakura shrine maiden amidst the ‘Land of Dreams,’ captivated by the nocturnal cherry blossoms’ radiance, her smile as dazzling as a fairytale protagonist’s. In the logotype, I’ve woven together elements from Sakura Miko’s past Anniversary and Birthday events, creating a nostalgic tapestry that reflects upon the past six years. Art by しゃふぁさん

miComet Lightbox Design

This piece holds a special place in my heart, marking our first foray into a dual lightbox display. One side is dedicated to Sakura Miko, and the other to Hoshimachi Suisei, together forming the miComet. The design draws inspiration from the track ‘シュガーラッシュ / miComet,’ with a palette of pinks and blues that evoke a blend of romance and mischief. Art by しずかさん

Hoshimachi Suisei Lightbox Design

Concluding the series is the ‘Starlight Shining in the Night Sky’ themed artwork, brought to life by キノkoさん. The comet, captured in this piece, bears a resolute gaze, its brilliance rivaling the pale blue stars that adorn the night sky. It’s a visual symphony that harmonizes with the star singer’s birthday aspirations, wishing for the comet’s luminescence to perpetually grace the avenues of VTubing and song. Art by キノkoさん
