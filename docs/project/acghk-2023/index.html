ACGHK 2023 Goods Designs ?

These are some Goods Designs for the HKvidol fan group to use at the ACG event "ACGHK 2023". With Design of A4 Folders, Postcards, and Keychains.

Graphics style and Color tone.

For each item, it has its own featured artwork by different artists / illustrators, It is important to pick up its own color style and elements to create similar graphics and designs that don't destroy its original theme and style.
