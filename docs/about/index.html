About 3s Design

“I D-Do funny things.”

3s Design, Independent Graphic Designer from Hong Kong. Specializing in Visual Storytelling & Branding Strategy .

In the beginning, I was captivated by Japanese graphic design—the spacing, the fonts, the compositions, the colors, and, most importantly, the meticulous attention to detail. After spending a few years in the design industry, I discovered that the essential components of design are typography, layout, and composition.

Practice

→ I Design for Love. I started designing in 2018. I make eye-catching Visuals to tell a story. Attention to detail, but keep it simple and clean.

I am a challenger working independently and/or collaborating with other professionals.
